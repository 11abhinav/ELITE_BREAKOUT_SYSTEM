# ELITE BREAKOUT SYSTEM - BUSINESS LOGIC SPECIFICATION

> **Note:** This document serves as the absolute canonical source of truth for the system's trading logic, scanners, scoring mechanics, and risk management algorithms. It completely details the business rules separate from the technical architecture.

## 1. Scanner Specifications

The system runs four distinct scanners, each targeting a specific market regime and structural setup. All scanners operate on the `Fundamental Watchlist` generated by the `Wealth Engine` to ensure base quality.

### 1.1 EOD Breakout Scanner
**Focus:** Daily momentum breakouts. Holds days to weeks.
**Execution:** Runs end-of-day (21:00 - 23:59 IST).
**Funnel & Hard Gates:**
1. **Minimum Price:** ₹100
2. **Minimum Liquidity:** Requires minimum average daily volume (e.g. > 100k shares).
3. **Hard Disqualifiers (Instant Reject 0 Score):**
    - `ADX < 25`: Too weak/choppy. Needs established directional commitment.
    - `RSI Divergence`: Price making new highs while RSI makes lower highs over a 5-day lookback.
    - `Overextension`: Breaching Upper Bollinger Band with Volume Ratio < 2.0.
    - `Exhaustion`: 3 dojis or narrow range candles in the last 4 bars.
    - `Volume Climax Top`: Rejection upper wick > 40% of range on climax volume.
**Signal Generation:**
- Requires fresh MACD Bullish Crossover.
- Reclaiming of 20 EMA / 50 SMA.
- Detects Tight Consolidation Base breakouts.

### 1.2 Multi-Timeframe (Multi-TF) Scanner
**Focus:** Intraday momentum transitioning into swing trades.
**Execution:** Intraday schedule. 
**Logic Structure:**
- **Phase A (Hourly Filter):** Scans the universe on a 1H timeframe. Requires trend permission: Price > 200 EMA, EMAs in correct stacking order (9 > 20 > 50), and ADX > 20. Symbols that pass are added to an `HOURLY_APPROVED` watchlist.
- **Phase B (15-Minute Hunting):** Scans only the `HOURLY_APPROVED` watchlist on a 15-minute timeframe. Looks for fresh base breakouts or volume surges.
- **Data Handling:** Explicitly strips "forming" (incomplete) candles based on strict IST timezone validation to prevent look-ahead bias.

### 1.3 Reversal Scanner (Mean Reversion)
**Focus:** High-quality, fundamentally strong stocks recovering from deep discounts.
**Execution:** End of Day.
**Key Constraints:**
- **Strict Trend Structure (v6):** A close `> SMA50` is now **MANDATORY**. The scanner requires a confirmed recovery structure, not just a falling-knife bounce.
- **Discount Band:** Drop from 52-week high must be within 20% to 45% (lowered to 15% for ultra-quality compounders/blue chips).
- **RSI Curl:** Must have recently been deep oversold (RSI < 25) in the last 15 bars, and now recovering/curling up.
- **MACD Cross:** Must have a fresh MACD Bullish Cross within the last 10 bars.
- **Cooldown Penalty:** Symbols are strictly suppressed for N days if they recently triggered a reversal alert that stopped out/failed.

### 1.4 Pullback Continuation Scanner (Pipeline V8-PB)
**Focus:** Orderly pullbacks in established, strong uptrends.
**Execution:** Nightly, orchestrated pipeline pattern.
**Logic Phases:**
- **Phase A (Eligibility):** Established uptrend (Close > SMA50 > SMA200).
- **Phase B (Impulse & Orderly Pullback):** 
  - Finds prior strong impulse (e.g. > 8% gain, > 3.0x ATR multiple).
  - Measures the pullback: Must be between 5% and 15% depth, lasting 3 to 20 bars.
  - **Disorderly Checks:** Rejects if more than 2 internal swing lows (choppy) or if volume isn't contracting (Max volume ratio < 0.75x).
- **Phase C (Resumption Trigger):** Rejects bearish closes, wicks > 40%, gaps > 3%, or failure to take out the prior bar's high. Requires bullish engulfing or strong close location (> 60%).

## 2. The Scoring Engine (0 - 100 Scale)

Every candidate that passes a scanner's hard gates is scored out of 100. Alerts are only generated if the score exceeds the regime-adjusted minimum threshold (usually 78-82 for EOD, 62 for Reversals).

### 2.1 EOD / Momentum Breakout Scoring
- **Category Weight (30 pts):** Based on fundamental tier (e.g., Debt-Free Cash Generator = 30, Wealth Compounder = 30, Blue Chip Stable = 14, Recovery Play = 8).
- **Breakout Signals (25 pts):** Awards 8 pts per technical signal (cap 24) + 1 pt bonus for 52W High breakout.
- **RSI Quality (15 pts):** Sweet spot is 60–70 (15 pts). 57–59 (6 pts). 70–74 (10 pts). Drops to 0 outside these bands.
- **Volume Surge (20 pts):** Escalating points for volume expansion (e.g. > 4.0x = 20 pts, > 3.0x = 15 pts, 1.5x = 3 pts).
- **Trend Strength (10 pts):** Moving Average alignment + ADX strength.

### 2.2 Reversal Specific Scoring
- **Trend Structure (25 pts max):** Above SMA50 & SMA200 = 25 pts. Above SMA50 only = 18 pts. Below SMA50 = 10 pts (soft pass, offset by MACD/EMA).
- **SMA200 Proximity (15 pts max):** Closer to the SMA200 scores higher (less falling-knife risk).
- **Volume Confirmation (15 pts max):** Volume is a secondary confirming indicator for reversals, unlike breakouts.
- **MACD Momentum (15 pts max):** Normalizes MACD Histogram by ATR to judge momentum strength.
- **RSI Curl Quality (15 pts max):** Higher points for explosive recovery from deep oversold limits.
- **Category Quality (10 pts max):** Same as above, heavily favoring quality.
- **Drop Sweet Spot (5 pts max):** Bonus points for falling exactly into the 25%-40% drop pocket.

## 3. Stop-Loss (SL) & Target Engine (Anti-Trap V5)

The system uses advanced SL placement to avoid "Operator Stop Hunts." Operators know retail places SL precisely at the exact swing low and sweep it before reversing.

### 3.1 Anti-Trap Stop-Loss Placement
- **Multi-Swing Clustering:** Scans recent swing lows. If 2+ cluster within 1% of each other, it groups them into a highly-reliable support zone.
- **Dynamic Anchoring:** 
  - Priority: Swing Low Cluster > True Pivot Swing Low > S1 > Rolling Swing Low > S2.
  - In intraday (Multi-TF), VWAP is used as an institutional fair-value support anchor if between the low and the entry price.
- **ATR / Percentage Buffers:** 
  - The final SL is placed *below* the anchor by `max(mode_atr_fraction * ATR, mode_pct * price)`.
  - **EOD Buffer:** max(0.75x ATR, 0.75%).
  - **Reversal Buffer:** max(1.0x ATR, 1.00%). (Wider to accommodate volatility).
- **ADX-Aware Widening:** Stocks in strong trends (ADX > 35) receive a 30% wider SL buffer to survive deeper natural pullbacks without being swept.

### 3.2 Target Placement & Caps
Targets are aggregated using a `CandidateGenerator` that builds levels from:
- Equal Highs (Liquidity sweeps)
- R1 / R2 / Resistance Pivots
- Fibonacci Extensions (1.272, 1.618, 2.0)
- ABCD measured moves
- Round Numbers (Dynamic offsetting: front-runs round numbers by a tick margin).
**ATR-Scaled Target Caps:** Prevents unrealistic fantasy targets.
- 15m capped at `5x ATR`.
- 1H capped at `8x ATR`.
- EOD capped at `12x ATR`.

## 4. The Wealth Engine (Fundamentals)

The Wealth Engine runs independently, analyzing financial data (screener/fundamentals) to construct the permitted universe. 
- Analyzes ROE, Debt-to-Equity, YoY Revenue Growth, Profit Margins, and Promoter Pledge.
- Generates categorized tiers.
- A stock with `Debt-to-Equity = 0.0` and `ROE > 20%` becomes a "Debt-Free Cash Generator".
- High Promoter Pledge (>10%) incurs a Bayesian penalty on the final scanner score.
- **Forensic Risk Tier:** Filters out highly questionable financials (REJECT tier).

## 5. Macro Regime & Strategy Policy (Bayesian Adaption)

The system automatically flexes its strictness based on broader market conditions (Nifty 20-day returns).
- **STRONG_BEAR:** Halts all Pullback/Breakout scanners entirely.
- **BEAR / CHOPPY:** Increases ADX thresholds, demands tighter stop-losses, restricts the maximum number of new positions per day.
- **BULL / TRENDING:** Loosens ADX bounds, accepts softer breakouts, increases the allowed target multipliers (Fib 200 gate opens).
- **Momentum Bonus Injection:** Candidates receive bonus score points if their specific Sector is currently in a "TAILWIND" regime or if their Relative Strength (RS) rating is > 80 percentile.
