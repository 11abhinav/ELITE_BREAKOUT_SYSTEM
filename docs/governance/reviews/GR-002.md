## 1. Specification vs. Implementation

**Current Specification (Constitution):**
"A valid reversal setup must occur after a minimum 15% drawdown from the 52-week high, and the daily MACD histogram must be positive (tick higher than previous day)."

**Current Implementation (`app/reversal_scanner.py`):**
The code treats MACD as a scoring factor and requires a bullish MACD crossover within the last 10 days, but does *not* mandate a strictly positive MACD histogram on the current day as a hard gate.

## 2. Evidence & Findings

- **Code Location:** `app/reversal_scanner.py:171` (Scoring) and `:673` (10-day lookback crossover).
- **Historical Context:** Commit history (`REVERSAL_PATCH_v1.0` / `FINDING-3 FIX`) shows the MACD crossover window was intentionally widened from 5 to 10 bars because mean-reversion setups develop slower. A strict MACD gate on the current day was explicitly found to reject 60% of valid setups where the crossover happened days prior and the stock is still following through.
- **Operational Impact:** The codebase allows valid but slightly delayed setups to pass, while the Constitution demands an exact day-of-crossover precision.

## 3. Governance Assessment

**Classification:** Specification/Implementation Drift

**Questions for Review:**
1. Does the historical backtest (which supported widening the MACD lookback window) validate that the scoring approach is superior to the hard gate?
2. If yes, should the Constitution be amended (Level 4 Change) to reflect a wider 10-day lookback requirement without the strict positive histogram veto?

## 4. Decision
**Status:** Pending Review
**Recommendation:** Highly likely requires a Level 4 Specification Change to align the Constitution with the backtest-proven 10-day lookback logic.
