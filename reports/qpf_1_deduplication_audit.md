## 3. Key Distinctions From Previous Research

### 3.1 Not a Value Recovery Screen
Unlike `QUALITY_COMPOUNDER_VALUE_V2`, which requires a stock to be historically cheap ($\ge 25\%$ EV/EBITDA discount) before purchasing, QPF-1 does not require undervaluation. It holds the highest quality compounders even when they trade at standard or elevated valuation multiples, relying on superior return on capital and steady dividend compounding to drive excess returns.

### 3.2 Not a Bear-Regime Rescue Vehicle
Unlike `BEAR_QUALITY_RECOVERY_V1`, which was designed as a specialized hedge to accumulate beaten-down stocks during market panics, QPF-1 is designed as a core, standing wealth allocation engine operating across Bull, Sideways, and Bear cycles.

### 3.3 Novel Payout & Cash Flow Dimension
QPF-1 explicitly integrates the findings of the **IIM Ahmedabad (2022) Quality Factor Study**, which proved that profitability combined with sustainable shareholder payouts delivers enhanced risk-adjusted returns in Indian equities. It rewards companies with high CFO/PAT ratios and healthy distribution yields while penalizing debt-funded dividends.
