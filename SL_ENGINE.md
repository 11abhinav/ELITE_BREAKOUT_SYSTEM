# V6.2.2 Stop Loss Engine Architecture

*This is the canonical single source of truth for the Elite Breakout System's risk management engine. The architecture is formally frozen as of V6.2.2.*

---

## 1. Philosophy
The Stop Loss Engine is designed around a strict Separation of Concerns. It acts purely as a structural risk-definition engine, entirely isolated from Trade Quality, Position Sizing, or Momentum assessment.

The engine operates on the principle of **Predictive Structural Floors**: finding the strongest confluence of historical support below an entry price, mathematically anchoring to it, and applying a buffer scaled directly to both the strength of that floor and current market volatility.

Execution is strictly deterministic. There are no recovery checks, no volume-trap logic, and no close-basis waiting. If the absolute stop loss is breached by a single tick, the trade is terminated immediately.

---

## 2. Architecture & Component Ownership
To prevent logic sprawl, the system is strictly segmented into single-responsibility engines:

### `MarketStructure` Layer
- **`SupportEngine`**: Heavyweight component. Owns structural cluster creation, overlap scoring, and weighted anchor calculation for Stops. 
- **`ResistanceSelector`**: Lightweight component. Owns filtering and ranking structural ceilings by importance to define Targets. No clustering or weighting.

### `SL Generator` (Orchestrator)
**Owns:** Volatility-aware adaptive buffers, RR calculation, and Trade Validation (Accept/Reject).
**Never Owns:** Structural discovery. It purely consumes `SupportEngine` and `ResistanceSelector` outputs.

### `TradeQualityEngine` & `PortfolioEngine` (Upstream/Downstream)
- **Trade Quality** owns Momentum (MACD, RSI), Volume (ADX, expansion), and Reward/Risk ratios.
- **Portfolio Engine** owns Capital allocation and Position sizing.

## 3. Public Interfaces
The `SupportEngine` provides a stateless public interface `get_strongest_support(entry, eff_atr, supports)`.
It returns a rich diagnostic object, consumed by the SL Generator and Validation tools:
```json
{
  "score": 68,
  "anchor_price": 95.18,
  "cluster_strength": "HIGH",
  "anchor_confidence": 0.68,
  "cluster_width": 0.42,

    {"type": "SMA200", "price": 95.2, "score": 30},
    {"type": "SWING_LOW", "price": 95.0, "score": 38}

}
```

## 4. Configuration (`config.py`)
The engine's rules are governed by the `STRUCTURAL_STOP` dictionary:
- `MAX_CLUSTER_WIDTH_ATR`: Defines the maximum width of a valid support cluster (default: `1.5 ATR`).
- `DISASTER_BUFFER_PCT`: Floor percentage for fallback disaster stops.
- `SCORES`: Base scores for individual support elements (e.g. Swing Low Cluster = 40, SMA200 = 30, EMA20 = 15).
- `BONUS_OVERLAP`: Score added for structural confluence (default: `+15`).

---

## 5. Algorithms

### Clustering & Scoring
1. All valid technical supports within `1.5 ATR` of the highest local support form a cluster.
2. The cluster receives a **Final Support Score** equal to the sum of its members' Base Scores plus the Overlap Bonus. 
3. **No momentum or volume data is factored into the Support Score.**

### Weighted Anchor Calculation
The Anchor Price is calculated as the **Weighted Average** of the cluster's members, weighted by their individual Final Support Scores. This ensures the anchor is naturally magnetized toward the strongest structural confluence within the cluster.

### Adaptive Buffer Generation
The final Stop Loss is calculated as: `Anchor Price - (Base Multiplier * Quality Modifier * ATR)`

**Volatility (Base Multiplier):**
- Low Volatility (`ATR < 2%`): `0.5x ATR`
- Normal Volatility: `0.75x ATR`
- High Volatility (`ATR > 6%`): `1.0x ATR`

**Quality Modifier:**
- Strong Support (`Score > 60`): `0.8` (Tighter stop)
- Weak Support (`Score < 30`): `1.2` (Wider stop)
- Normal Support: `1.0`

### Explicit Disaster Stop
The disaster stop follows a strict hierarchy:
`max(Primary SL - 1.0 ATR, Nearest Lower Major Support)`
If a lower structural floor exists, it is explicitly used as the disaster level. It never floats unanchored.

## 6. Validation
The engine is continuously validated via `validation_report.py`. 
The report aggregates the JSON `sl_result` metadata generated by the engine to output a **Support Score Predictability Matrix**, correlating:
`Support Score Bracket (<30, 30-40, 40-60, >60)` ➔ `Avg RR | Avg Reward % | Win % | Avg Holding Days`
This allows direct empirical observation of whether stronger support structures lead to superior trade outcomes.

---

## 7. Freeze Governance
The V6.2.2 Stop Loss Architecture is **strictly feature-frozen**. 
- No new indicators may be added.
- No new support types may be added.
- No new scoring bonuses may be added.
- No execution flows may be changed.

**Exceptions**: Parameter tuning (e.g., tweaking `BONUS_OVERLAP` or base scores) and bug fixes are permitted **ONLY IF** validated against ≥100 completed trades (or a predefined statistically meaningful sample) showing:
1. A measurable improvement in Win Rate, Expectancy, or Reward/Risk.
2. No material increase in Maximum Drawdown.

**Any fundamental architectural change requires a new engine version (e.g., V7.0) rather than modifying V6.2.2 in place.**
