📖 Wealth Engine Trading Guide

Built on fundamental integrity and business durability.

1. The V5 Pipeline (Quality Filters)

Every stock evaluated by the engine must pass the brutal V5 Pipeline, scored across 5 dimensions: Business Quality, Growth Velocity, Financial Strength, Valuation, and Market Structure.

2. Entry Rules (Dual-Gate & Sector Caps)

To generate an active BUY alert, a stock must survive a brutal multi-layer elimination process. Out of thousands of stocks, it must strictly pass all of these rules:

  • Bucket Prerequisite: It MUST pass the rigorous fundamental filters to land in a specific bucket (e.g. ROCE > 20% for Core, or YoY Growth > 20% for Growth).
  • Hard Quantitative Caps: It must rank mathematically in the absolute Top 15 Core, Top 10 Growth, Top 10 Opp, or Top 5 QoS stocks in the entire market. It enforces a strict 2-stock limit per industry.
  • Value + Momentum Check: It must maintain a Fundamental Score ≥ 55 and confirm active institutional buying with a Momentum Score ≥ 25 (above the 200 SMA).
  • Position Guard: If the stock is already actively open in your portfolio, it will be suppressed.
3. Portfolio Buckets
  • 🏆 Core Compounders (Score ≥ 65): Market Cap > ₹10,000 Cr, ROCE > 20%, Low Debt. Allocate the majority of your long-term capital here.
  • 🚀 Growth Multipliers (Score ≥ 60): Market Cap > ₹2,000 Cr, >20% YoY Growth. Emerging leaders. Higher risk, massive alpha. Allocate a smaller chunk here.
  • 🔥 Opportunistic Momentum (Score ≥ 55): YoY Profit > 40%, RS > 15. Massive acceleration and high momentum trades.
  • 💎 Quality-On-Sale (Score ≥ 50): Elite companies temporarily discounted (PEG < 1.0) with early momentum recovery. Usually triggers during market dips.
4. Position Sizing (Alloc %)

Do not guess your position size! The dashboard automatically calculates the Alloc % using the stock's ATR (Volatility) and Mom Score. It sizes down volatile assets to mathematically normalize risk and sizes up massive momentum. Do not override this.

5. Managing Open Positions (Hold Score)

Your open trades are monitored daily using a dynamic Hold Score (0-100):

  • Stable (> 60): Fundamentals and technicals are intact. Keep holding.
  • SELL REVIEW (< 45): Fundamentals have decayed or support broken. Prepare to exit.
  • Catastrophic Stop: If a stock drops > 20% from entry, the system bypasses all logic and triggers an immediate SELL. Cut the loss.
  • TLH (Tax Loss Harvest): Recommends selling a losing stock just before the 1-year mark to offset realized gains for tax purposes.
6. Key Data Columns
  • Mom Score: Measures institutional buying pressure. High score = immediate upside probability.
  • RS (6M): Relative Strength vs Nifty. Positive means it's beating the index.
